Common questions

A render failed. Was I charged a makeover? No. A failed render never costs a makeover — the app cannot deduct one unless a picture actually came back.
I bought makeovers on my old phone. How do I get them back? If you signed in with Apple or Google when you bought them, sign in again in Settings and they return. If you tapped "Not now", the packs were tied to that phone; email us with your Your ID and the receipt and we will sort it out.
How do I cancel my subscription? Settings → Manage subscription takes you straight to the App Store or Google Play page where you cancel. Cancelling keeps Pro until the day you have paid for ends. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in the store.
Where do my photos go? Your photo is sent to our server only to be rendered and is deleted within an hour. Nothing is kept. Everything you make lives on your phone. See the privacy policy.
How do I delete everything? Settings → Delete my data. It lists exactly what goes, asks you to type DELETE, and then removes it all — including your sign-in account if you made one.
