Stylist AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Stylist AI (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an AI interior design app. You photograph a room, choose a style, and the app returns a redesigned version of that room. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address or phone number. Your photos are sent to our image-processing provider to produce your design and are not used to train any model by us. Your finished designs are stored on your own device, not on our servers.

Deleting your data

This is first because it is the thing people most often want and most often cannot find.

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my data. It lists exactly what will go, asks you to type DELETE, and then erases your designs, your setup answers, your anonymous identifier and — if you made one — your sign-in account, all in one step. Nothing is kept back.
  • Your designs and your setup answers live on your device. Deleting the app also removes them immediately and completely. There is no copy on our servers to chase.
  • Your finished design is deleted from our image provider automatically within one hour of being made. You do not have to ask, and you cannot forget to.
  • The anonymous request log on our server can be deleted on request. Email elevatelogiclabs@gmail.com with the “Your ID” shown on the app’s Settings screen and we will remove it within 30 days. Usage events already sent to PostHog carry only that identifier, which “Delete my data” resets.

1. Information we collect

Photos you choose to upload

When you create a design, the photo you select is transmitted to our server, which passes it to our AI image-processing provider (fal.ai) to generate your result. The photo is used solely to produce that result. We do not retain your photos on our servers after processing, and we do not use your photos or your designs to train any model. Your finished design is set to expire at our provider within one hour, on every single request.

Our provider processes the image under its own terms and privacy policy. Please review fal.ai’s privacy policy if you would like to understand their handling of transmitted content.

Your answers to the setup questions

During setup the app asks about your taste — room types, styles, colours, materials, budget and similar. These answers describe preferences, not identity. They are stored on your device and are used to personalise your designs and the app.

Your designs

Finished designs are saved to your device in the app’s Lookbook. They are not uploaded to our servers. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

Purchase information

Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat and by Apple or Google. We receive an anonymous identifier and your subscription status (active or expired). We never see your payment card details — those are handled entirely by the app store.

The optional sign-in, when you buy a makeover pack

You never need an account to use the app. The one time we offer a sign-in is when you buy a pack of makeovers, so the pack can be restored if you change phones — and you can decline it. If you do sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and, where the provider shares it, your email address. We use the email for nothing except showing you which account holds your packs and restoring them; we do not send mail to it. Deleting your data deletes this account too.

Technical information

Our server records the anonymous identifier and the time of each design request, in order to apply fair-use limits and prevent abuse. This is not linked to any identity.

Usage and crash information

So that we can see where the app is confusing and when it breaks, we record a small set of events — for example the app was opened, a design was requested, the subscription screen was shown, a purchase was made — and, if the app crashes, a technical report of where in the code it happened. These are processed by PostHog (usage) and Sentry (crashes). They never include your photos, your designs, the text you type, your name or your email. The only identifier attached is the same anonymous one shown as “Your ID” in Settings, so that a purchase can be matched to the screens that led to it. We do not record your screen, and we do not use any of this for advertising. Choosing “Delete my data” resets this identifier so nothing further can be connected to you.

What we do not collect

  • No name, postal address or phone number — and no email address unless you choose the optional sign-in when buying a pack, as described above
  • No password, ever
  • No contacts, location, health or financial data
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party ad tracking

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Photo library — so you can choose a room photo. We access only the photo you pick.
  • Camera — only if you choose to take a photo instead of selecting one.
  • Saving to your photo library — only when you tap save on a finished design.
  • Notifications — optional, and only if you turn them on. Used to tell you a design is ready, or to nudge you if you left the two-minute setup unfinished. Never used for advertising, and never sent from a server — your phone schedules them itself, so no record of you exists anywhere.

You can change any of these at any time in your device settings, and the app continues to work without them (except where a photo is obviously required).

3. How we use the information

  • To generate the design you asked for
  • To personalise styles and suggestions to your stated taste
  • To keep track of how many designs remain on your plan
  • To apply fair-use limits and prevent automated abuse
  • To fix problems and improve the app

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not build profiles about you for marketing by third parties.

4. Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase — hosts the server that receives your design request.
  • fal.ai — performs the AI image generation.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status.
  • PostHog — counts the usage events described above (hosted in the United States).
  • Sentry — receives crash reports (hosted in the United States).
  • Apple / Google — process payments, distribute the app, and provide the optional sign-in.

5. Keeping data

Your photo is sent inside the request itself rather than uploaded and filed, so there is no stored copy of it on our servers. Your finished design is deleted from our image provider within one hour. We set that expiry on every request, so it does not depend on anyone remembering to run a clean-up. The app saves your design to your own device within seconds of it being made, so nothing of yours is lost when it expires.

Anonymous request logs are kept for a short period for abuse prevention. Everything else — your answers, your designs — lives on your device and is under your control.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold an account for you, most of your data is already in your hands: deleting the app removes your answers and your designs from your device. If you are in the UK, EU, California or another region with data protection rights and you would like to exercise them regarding anything we do hold, contact us using the address below. We will need the anonymous identifier shown in the app’s Settings screen to locate anything associated with your installation.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, those transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Security

Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted in transit. Our AI provider’s credentials are held only on our server and are never included in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store the material that would be most sensitive if it were exposed.

10. The AI models we use

Your design is produced by one of two Google image models, reached through fal.ai. Which one is used depends on the tool you picked, not on what you pay:

  • Nano Banana — exterior & facade, new walls, new flooring, declutter, furnish an empty room
  • Nano Banana Pro — interior redesign, garden & landscape, outdoor living, style transfer

We name them because you are entitled to know what is looking at a photograph of your home. If we change models, this list changes with them.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data — for example, if we add analytics or introduce optional accounts — we will update this page and change the date above. Material changes will be signalled in the app.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: elevatelogiclabs@gmail.com
