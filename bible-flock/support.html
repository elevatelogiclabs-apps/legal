Common questions

How do I delete my account? If you signed in with Apple: You › Account and data › Delete account. We delete your account, your backup and your Apple sign-in token from our server, and cancel Sign in with Apple. This can’t be undone.

If you never signed in, what you keep is on your phone: You › Account and data › Erase everything on this phone. Your quiet account on our server has no name or email; write to us and we will remove it.

Deleting your account does not cancel Plus. Cancel it in your Apple ID’s subscriptions first (below).

How do I cancel Plus? Plus is billed by Apple, so you cancel it in your Apple ID’s subscriptions: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Bible Flock and tap Cancel Subscription. In the app, Help › Cancel my subscription takes you there.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, or of the free trial, so that it does not renew. Cancelling keeps Plus open until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are made by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com; we cannot issue them ourselves. Sign in there with the Apple ID you paid with and choose the purchase. In the app, Help › Refunds and billing takes you there.
