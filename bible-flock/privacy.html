Bible Flock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short

  • Most of what you do in Bible Flock stays on your phone.
  • We keep a copy of your verses, notes and plans only if you sign in, so they come back on a new phone.
  • We never sell your data. There are no ads and no advertising trackers.
  • What you write is never part of our usage numbers or crash reports.
  • Once you sign in, you can delete your account from inside the app, and we delete what we hold.

1. Who we are

Bible Flock is made by Elevate Logic Labs LLC, a company registered in the United States. For anything about your privacy, write to elevatelogiclabsapps@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

  • The names you write: your name on You, and the name on a verse you send (“For …”). These never leave your phone.
  • Where you are in the Bible, your week, your settings and your reminders. Reminders are set on your phone; we do not send them.
  • Your loved and saved verses, your notes and your reading plans, unless you sign in to back them up (section 3).

3. What we keep on our server

A private, anonymous account. When you first open the app, it makes a quiet account with a random number and no name or email. It lets you send us a note and, later, back up what you keep.

If you sign in with Apple: what Apple gives us — an identifier, and your email address (or Apple’s private relay address, if you choose to hide yours). We keep a token from Apple only so that we can cancel the sign-in with Apple when you delete your account.

Your backup, once you sign in: your loved and saved verses, your notes and your plan progress, so they come back on a new phone.

A note you send us from Help › Send us a note: its kind (an idea, a problem…), your words, and the app’s version. If you delete your account later, the note stays with us but is no longer linked to you.

Your subscription: whether Plus is active, from Apple, through our subscription provider. We never see your card or bank details.

4. Usage numbers and crash reports

  • Anonymous usage numbers tell us that something happened in the app — a page opened, a verse loved — never what you wrote. They are on at first; turn them off any time in You › Share anonymous usage.
  • Crash reports tell us when the app stops, so we can fix it: the device, the iOS version, the app version and the error, with your words removed.

5. What we never do

We never sell or rent your data. We show no ads and use no advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not build a profile of you for anyone.

6. The companies that help us

Each one gets only what it needs to do its job for us:

  • Supabase — our server: the anonymous account, your backup once you sign in, your notes to us.
  • Apple — the App Store, Sign in with Apple and payments.
  • RevenueCat — keeps track of your subscription.
  • PostHog — the anonymous usage numbers.
  • Sentry — the crash reports.

If we add a company that handles your personal data, we will update this page first.

7. Where your data is kept

Our providers keep data on their servers, which may be in another country than yours, such as the United States. Where the law asks for it, we rely on standard safeguards for moving data between countries.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account and your backup: for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account: we delete your account, your backup and your Apple sign-in token from our server, and cancel Sign in with Apple. Notes you sent us stay, without your account.
  • Crash reports and usage numbers: as long as our providers keep them for us, and no longer than we need them.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: You › Account and data › Delete account, once you have signed in. If you never signed in, your quiet account has no name or email; write to us and we will remove it.
  • Erase this phone: You › Account and data › Erase everything on this phone.
  • Take your things with you: Settings › Export (as text or a spreadsheet).
  • Turn off usage numbers: You › Share anonymous usage.
  • You can also write to elevatelogiclabsapps@gmail.com to see, correct or delete what we hold. Wherever you live, we will help; in the UK and the EU you may also complain to your data protection authority, and in California we confirm that we do not sell or share personal information as the CCPA defines it.

10. Children

Bible Flock is for adults: you must be 18 or older to use it. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Keeping it safe

Data travels to our server encrypted, and your sign-in is kept encrypted on your phone. Only the few people who run Bible Flock can reach the server, and only to keep it working. If something bad ever happens to your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will change the date at the top and tell you in the app.

13. Contact

Elevate Logic Labs LLC — elevatelogiclabsapps@gmail.com
